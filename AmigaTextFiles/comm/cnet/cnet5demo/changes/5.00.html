Changes for v5.0
Click here  for information on upgrading to CNet/5.

NOTE: Your CNet/4 BBSLICENSE will not work with CNet/5. Contact us in IRC or by E-Mail  for a new license.
  1. Force private subboards will no longer allow private messages to be addressed to nobody.
  2. Subboard carbons are now working for uploaded/adopted files.

  3. AutoSession® implemented.

    AutoSession allows users to terminate a previous session simply by re-connecting to a different port. The user will be prompted whether to terminate the previous session. If termination is desired, the previous session is terminated internally.

    If, after 12 seconds, the previous session cannot terminate, an auto-detach of all running doors is performed and an attempt to terminate for another 13 seconds.

    If, on failure of the complete 25 second termination event, the previous session cannot be terminated, the new session cannot be allowed to login and the user will be notified that they can only have 1 account per port.
