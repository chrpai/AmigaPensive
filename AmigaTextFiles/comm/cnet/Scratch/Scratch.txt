    Introduction.
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 Scratch is a very simple gambling door for CNet. It's a conversion of
 the Excelsior! door ScratchE! written by me.

 The aim of the game is to find at least three equal symbols/numbers in
 a scratch-ticket that has six fields.

 The game can be played in four different ways:

    1. Play for extra time.
    2. Play for extra download bytes.
    3. Play for extra download files.
    4. Play for fun.


    Installation.
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 Copy the files in this archive to a directory of your choice (for example
 to "pfiles:Scratch").

 Now go to the pfiles section on your BBS and add Scratch as a ARexx door.

 And... that's it! :))
