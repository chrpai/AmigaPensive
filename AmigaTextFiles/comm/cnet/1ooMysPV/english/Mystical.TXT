                                                          ...intro - - ---.
- - ----------------------------------------------------------------------'

   Long,  long  ago,  before  every  historical record, there was a time of
Legends...

   For centuries  the human  race kept  itself hidden in subterranean kaers 
while creatures from the  astral  dimension, horrors  as they  were called, 
covered the land with terror and destruction.  But now the long dark age of 
the plague  has come to an end and bold adventurers settle to reconquer the
land of their ancestors.  Elves,  Orcs,  T'skrang,  Humans and  other races
explore a  world of dangers  without names, strange creatures and  ruins of
cities,  full  of  ancient  secrets and  uncountable  treasures...  a world
dominated  by magic.  In songs and tales, these heroes, on their search for
the glamour of past days, become immortally.

   The heroes of today are the legends of tomorrow.


                -- - Mystical - - the time of legends - ---



                                             ...what is Mystical?? - - ---.
- - ----------------------------------------------------------------------'

   Mystical will be a fantasy role play  game for CNet/3 and higher that is
based on the roots, but  not the ideas of the quite popular game EarthDawn. 
The idea is  to create an on-line RPG that  features a complexity otherwise
only known  from Multiple User  Dungeons (MUDs) which  however are  in most
cases  unpretentious  when it comes to graphics. And, to create a role play
game with a story which really fills the player with enthusiasm instead  of
one  where all motivation  consists of killing monsters  to reach a  higher
level to kill even better monsters. In my eyes this has nothing to do with
real role playing and certainly not with fantasy!

   But  now  let  me  introduce the background of the game and what it will
offer. Enjoy!



                                         ...how everything started - - ---.
- - ----------------------------------------------------------------------'

   A  long  time  ago,  there  was a world  where all kinds of creatures we
otherwise only know from stories lived together. A peaceful planet with the
hugest forests and the bluest seas where everything was just terribly fine.

   Magicians  practiced  their  arts without knowing  that  magic also  has
another  side, a different  view on astral  dimensions. Every magician took
its energy  out of these dimensions  to accomplish  wonders,  without  even
thinking of the dangers that it might bring with it. In this parallel world
there was nothing but negative emotions. Creatures that spent most of their
life agonizing other beings. No, they did not kill them, they just tortured
them.

   After  a  few  years of peace, it came to the attention of these horrors
that humans on the other side  used powers from their side to perform their
daily tasks. Some horrors started using their astral  talents to mark these
magicians; to mark them  with a magic stamp  that allowed them to recognize
the intruders immediately at their next appearance.

   So,  after  a short period all magicians on the planets had been marked.
This was the time when magicians,  conjurers and cures, after  more or less
successfully  entering  the  astral  dimension,  were  put  to suffer  from
unbelievable pain. Alternately, the horrors tore their spirits so that only
their bodies remained in the existential world.

   At  first,  the  scholars  couldn't even imagine that horrors or similar
creatures even existed.  This was their  biggest mistakes. Horrors  did not 
really exist -- they  were astral  appearances, created by, as was believed
afterwards, the evil that lived inside of everyone.

   But  then  the  whole  land,  region  by region, suffered from a wave of
violence. Horrors appeared and set cities to fire, murdered, brought people
under their control and forced them to serve as slaves.

   Only  a small tribe in the upper north of the land recognized the danger
early enough and so their scholars started to create theories and developed
a method to escape the terror of the horrors. The idea was to create kaers,
huge subterranean vaults, were  all humans should find together. A group of
magicians could then  unite to create a  magic shield  so that  the horrors
would not have a chance to intrude.

   This plan was nearly  perfect.  The small Nordic  swarm that was  called 
"Arhog" soon realized  that all other people  were too busy to  escape from
the horrors and so no one else had a plan to really protect themselves.

   They made the other tribes an offer they could  not reject. They offered
to take them  all into the kaers,  under the condition that they would work
for the  Arhog and that  they would reward  them greatly  when the  horrors
would cease their  raids. They reached  to rule over the lands of all those
who accepted their help.

   A  big  price to pay, but ultimately nearly all tribes agreed since they
wanted to save their lives.

   Only  the  queen of the elves resisted.  She  thought it would be just a
trick of those humans  thirsty for power. It was at  that moment  when  the
elves began to split. Only a small amount remained loyal and stood with the
mighty Empress, the others sold themselves to the Arhog.

   When almost everyone had disappeared from  the planet's surface  the last
elves  realized that  they had  made the  most dangerous  decision of  their
life. Horrors  invaded also the forest  of the  elves to  terrorize the last
beings on the planet.

   Many elves  were killed until the queen and her assistants found a second
solution to escape the pain.  They recognized that  these horrors  nourished
themselves from the pain they brought to the people.  Horrors were attracted
by the innocence of the people. They were looking to destroy it.

   The  Elvin magician Trasric  developed with  this knowledge together with
the Empress  what was later called "the ritual of thorns". A spell that made
two inch long thorns grow out of the body of everyone involved. A spell that
brought a life  of pain to the elves  in which however  they were ignored by
the horrors as for these pain was a common feeling.

   This extremely painful genetic change  brought deliverance  to these last
elves. B ut they, too, had  paid a high price.  They kept their  freedom but
were stigmatized for the rest of their life.

   The plague raged for a total of 150 years -- a period which brought total
destruction to a world which previously only knew the art of creation.

   It changed everything...



                                                       ...Features - - ---.
- - ----------------------------------------------------------------------'

* A  maximum of  interactivity  through  multi-port-support  (Client/Server
  technique)  and  hundreds  of  computer-controlled  Non-Player-Characters
  (NPCs) which  move through  the whole world,  join the player, hinder him
  or just meet with him to talk.

* NPCs  that die disappear  from the game  play and  may even  be buried in 
  surrounding cities. Their death could even be revenged by related NPCs.

* All  characters  including the  character  controlled by  the player  are 
  determined by a high number of characteristics: six different attributes,
  social  behavior,  over  120  different  learnable   talents/skills  with
  different effects depending on the characters' discipline...

* Characteristics improve with a character's experience, by solving puzzles,
  killing enemies  or simply  correct  behavior, e.g.. Cooperativity or its
  opposite if the character prefers the negative side of life.

* Numerous weapons,  armor, supplemental items, magic artifacts and special
  items that can only be found  in adventures, which may be unique and thus
  of special value.

* A number of cities,  villages, dungeons and other locations of play which
  are arbitrarily extendable and modifyable offer the  player together with
  a complex landscape, consisting of mountains, rivers and forests, a world
  of unbelievable complexity which can be only be exceeded by a "real Pen &
  Paper" role playing game.

* In every town different  NPCs spend their daily life. Prices for weapons,
  armor etc. as well as the inventory itself can vary from town to town and
  village to village.

* There is no internal limit  as to how  long the game  can be played. Your
   users can play until their on-line time has exceeded.

* The game  is not  determined  by a number  of ficticious "lives". Instead 
  every  player has a health value.  For example,  if the  damage  suffered
  pushes a   player  beyond  a  certain  threshold  he   might  slip   into
  unconsciousness.  If the  damage is even  worse, the character might die.
  However,  dead  does not  necessarily  mean that it  is out  of the  game
  forever.

* The game play is based  on everyday  activities such as hunting, visiting
  arenas etc. but  also consists of  adventure modules  in which the player
  has to pass a certain story. These modules are extendable as well.

* The gross number of locations and events features colorful ANSI graphics.

* etc.



                                        ...so when will it be out? - - ---.
- - ----------------------------------------------------------------------'

   Most  probably  in  the  first quarter  of  1998.  The archive will then
consist of the following components:

 * the Server which takes  care of all data and text management and is also
   used to control game play.

 * the Client, the real heart of the game

 * databases with lots of items, NPCs, adventurers etc.

 * a lot of ANSIs



                                          ...what else is planned? - - ---.
- - ----------------------------------------------------------------------'

 * Update releases of databases and/or adventure modules.

 * A module which allows for data exchange in FIDO-based networks.
