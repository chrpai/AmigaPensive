     ----------------   BASIC AND COMMON COMMANDS   ----------------

        BRIEF - This command fully describe a location only the first
                time you enter it.  On subsequent visits only show the
                name of the Location.  (All adventures default to this)

   SUPERBRIEF - Displays only the -name- of a place you visit even if
                its your first time there of course you can always get
                a full description with the LOOK or L command.

      VERBOSE - Gives a complete description of the area EVERYTIME you
                enter it.

     DIAGNOSE - This will give you a report of your physical condition.

    INVENTORY - Displays a list of everything you are wearing and carry.
                This command can be abbreviated to I.

         LOOK - Gives a full description of location.  Abreviation to L.

         QUIT - This lets you stop playing. (See SAVE)

         SAVE - Allows you to 'snapshot' your current position and status
                in a game.  

      RESTORE - This restores a previously saved game.

      RESTART - This stops a story and starts it over from the beginning.

        SCORE - Shows current score and ranking

         TIME - Gives you the current time in the story (some games)

         WAIT - Causes time in the story to pass


               --------------- MOVING AROUND -------------------

     All directions can be abbreviated as follows:

     NORTH - N     EAST - E    SOUTH - S    WEST - W   UP - U   DOWN - D

     NorthWest - NW  NorthEast - NE etc and so on.


    Most command sentences require noun and verb here are some examples

>TAKE BIRDCAGE
>OPEN THE PANEL
>EXAMINE SHINY COIN
>READ ABOUT DIMWIT FLATHEAD
>LIE DOWN ON GREEN COUCH
>ASK FRED ABOUT THE TOWEL

    Examples of Multiple instruction using the word AND or a comma.

>TAKE THE BOOK AND THE FROG
>DROP THE JAR OF PEANUTS, THE SPOON, AND THE LEMMING FOOD
>PUT THE EGG AND THE PENCIL IN THE CABINET

    The word ALL can be used to save time ie >TAKE ALL.  Also there are
    three basic questions you can ask.. WHO IS, WHERE IS, WHAT IS.

    To speak to other characters type their name, then a comma, then
    whatever you want to say to them.

>JESTER, HELLO
>GUSTAR WOOMAX, TELL ME ABOUT THE COCONUT
>UNCLE OTTO, GIVE ME YOUR WALLET
>HORSE, WHERE IS YOUR SADDLE
>BOY, RUN HOME THEN CALL THE POLICE
>MIGHTY WIZARD, TAKE THIS POISONED APPLE. EAT IT


      ---------- FINALLY WHEN ALL ELSE FAILS -------------

   When all else fails and you get hopelessly STUCK in a game and
   can't solve a puzzle etc you can always ask your SYSOP for a few
   hints or tips or possibly the solution if your really hopeless!

   Its a GOOD idea to have a pencil and pad handy to MAP out the
   games locations some game you'll NEED to do this to complete.

   Good luck and happy adventuring!
