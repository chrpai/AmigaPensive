		Changes from the last version to this one (v4.0)
		________________________________________________

     ********************************************************************
     *** NOTE:  It is reccommended that you erase all your data files ***
     ***        and re-start the game over when upgrading from v3- to ***
     ***        this version.  Lots of changes have been made!        ***
     ********************************************************************

 ->  You no longer have to type in the user's alias to send a message, spy,
     or hack another user.  It will list out the players and allow you to
     pick a player by number.  It is hotkeyed, and allows up to 99 players.

 ->  The HAA has been totaly given a face lift and re-done a bit; you may now
     buy AND sell your equipment and terminal software.  Selling it will get
     you half of what you originaly paid for it.  The HAA has been hotkeyed
     as well.

 ->  The "USER" player listing now shows real names for sysops (Their alias on
     the BBS that is).

 ->  The main directory is a textfile now and is MUCH faster.

 ->  The messaging system has been re-done.  Players no longer have 2 files
     in the "MESSAGES" directory, just one.

 ->  "CD DIAL", and from there "CD ROOT" are now free commands.  Don't know why
     they wern't before. Hmph!

 ->  The play counter now works in the terminal directory as well as the main.
     This was because people would sit there and crash boards all day, and
     could theoretically "win" the game in one call.
