                         L U N A R   C O N Q U E S T

                                    V2.00+

                                Hints and Tips

                                   08/10/96

On your very first turn, sell everything but Mines and Food.  If possible,
buy extra Food.  Buy half as much as you have on hand, but keep about
10,000 Creds on hand for station expenses.  In the Administration Phase
(when you have to pay out), pay 125% of the amount requested.  In the
Options, change your Tax Rate to 9%.

Buy as much Food as you can afford every time the price falls to 1 Cred and
sell it when the price hits 4 or 5 Creds.

If you can buy a good amount of Ore, one good trade will set you up for the
rest of the game.

Don't forget to buy more Mines whenever you can.

Jack your Tax Rate up to 100% for a turn for some quick Creds.  DON'T
FORGET TO LOWER IT AGAIN!

For some serious combat action, use your LaserSAT against your opponent's
Countermissiles.  Then use a Full Assault.  The first action will be
Missile attack, which will be more effective, since you just eliminated the
Countermissiles.
