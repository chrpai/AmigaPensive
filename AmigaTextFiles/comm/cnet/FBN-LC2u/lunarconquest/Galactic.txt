What Is Galactic?
-----------------

When playing the Galactic version of Lunar Conquest (The word `Galaxy'
is on the title page above the version number.), you can launch assaults
against users on other solar systems (BBSes) if their SysOp is smart enough
to get a Galactic version of LC.

How Is It Different From The Solar System Version?
--------------------------------------------------

You will have to support a force of Space Raiders in your administration
phase.  You will have to construct a Space Cruiser for transporting your
forces to other solar systems.  There is a Galactic option in the options
menu.

Space Raiders
-------------

These are your defense forces.  They are your primary means of defense
against incursions from other solar systems.  Satellite Killers, Missiles
and your LaserSAT are also used for defense from extrasolar attackers.

Space Cruiser
-------------

Once you've constructed a Space Cruiser, you can use it to send Lunar
Rovers, Tanks and Hoverjets to other solar systems.

Galactic Options
----------------

The Space Armada is the way to pool your resources with others in your
solar system.  This allows for a much larger attack force to be launched,
with a greater chance of success.  The Space Armada attacks entire solar
systems.

First, a Space Transport must be constructed.  Then, players contribute
Lunar Rovers, Tanks and Hoverjets.  After a certain length of time, the
Space Armada is launched against its target.

The Galactic version of LC adds a whole new dimension of play to an already
exciting game.  Ask your SysOp to get the Galactic version of LC today!
