You've created an account, now what?  Very simple - KILL!

Lunar Conquest is a game of combat, plain and simple.  You, and your
opponents, are attempting to gain control of the resources on the Moon.  To
that end, you have troops, workers, spies and equipment to deploy to best
effect.

You will be under protection for several turns.  Use it wisely.  Your best
bet is to sell off everything but your mines and food.  There's no sense
paying for military when you are in protection.  Don't forget to buy some
before protection runs out!

Each day, you will have some ComOp, EspOp and TransOp points.  These are,
respectively, Combat, Espionage and Transfer points.  This limits the
number of missions you can undertake in one day.  Note that some missions
cost MORE than one point.

The LUNA1 Commodities Market is where you make your money.  Food and Ore
prices fluctuate each turn, all other commodities' prices fluctuate daily.
Buying Food and Ore low and selling high is the order of the day.

Your goal is to get all the mines.  Remember!  Anyone can be taken down.
Having lots of mines and troops does not make you invincible.  A trip to
Mr. Black's Market can ruin someone's day.  Espionage can take someone's
support down too low to recover.  LaserSAT combat can wipe out even the most
powerful of opponents.

Good luck.
