              A Configuration for Supra 9600/14,400 FAX/DATA Modems


      I've been pleased with both Supra modems I've owned. I recently
upgraded from the 9600v.32 to the 14,400 v.32bis. I had the 9600 for about 2
months and it ALWAYS worked great in AT&F2 configuration. My 14,400v.32bis
is just as trouble-free. This text is written to customize and IMPROVE the
on-line performance of both 9600 v.32 and 14,400 v.32bis modems. I get regular
cps rates on compressed files (lha,lzh) of around 1700 cps. On DMS files it
runs around 1900 cps average. Text files are over 3000 cps!! (whew!) I have
improved the performance of my modem thru configuration. The configuration
I've found to be most reliable (few if ANY errors) and fastest is:

 1) Issue AT&F2 --> Clears NRAM and resets to factory configuration so you
                    KNOW your current active configuration. AT&F2 is the
                    BEST all-around modem configuration. Except for the
                    'too loud at any setting' speaker. ATM turns it off.
                    (for more on AT&F2 see pgs 21 and 22 in Reference
                     Manual)

 2) Issue AT%S250-> Increases the # of characters that can be compressed
                    onto one word from 32 (default) to 250. This directly
                    increases modem through put.(See pg 43 in Reference
                    Manual).

 3) Issue AT\A3 --> Increases MNP block size from 128 characters(default)
                    to 256. This directly increases modem through put.(See
                    pg 41 in Reference Manual).

 4) Issue AT&Q9 --> Enables fall-back modes starting with v.42bis and then
                    falling back thru v.42 and normal modes during connect-
                    ion negotiations. This gives more flexibility when
                    connecting and will guarantee the best compression mode
                    available to BOTH modems will be selected once negotia-
                    tion is completed.

 5) Issue AT&S11 -> This merely speeds up the time it takes to dial out.
                    No on-line performance advantage just a little quicker
                    in dialing giving you an increase in # of attempts
                    per minute.( i.e. getting 'in' on a busy BBS)
                    (See pg 48 in Reference Manual)

 6) Issue ATW1----> Enables 2 ADDITIONAL connect result codes on your
                    screen after a connection is made. The PROTOCOL and
                    CARRIER rate are now displayed in addition to the
                    normal CONNECT 14400.(See pg 45 in Reference Manual)

 7) Issue ATS95=32  Enables the COMPRESSION type (i.e. v.42bis) to be dis-
                    played along with everything else in mumber 6 above.
                    After each connection your screen will now display
                    4 lines of information instead of just 1 like the
                    default you get with using AT&F2 configuration. This is
                    personal preference only and doesn't speed up your
                    modem in any way. It does give you more information
                    about the negotiated connection. (See p 45 in Reference
                    Manual for 6 and 7 both)

 8) Issue ATM ----> Turns speaker off permanently. Just in case you're
                    tired of all the screeching noise during negotiations.
                    Some prefer speaker on!! 

 9) Issue AT&W0 --> This SAVES everything we've changed to the modem's
                    stored configuration RAM. This RAM is the one that
                    is loaded by default when you turn your modem on.
                    

 Use Workbench 2.04/2.1 instead of Workbench 1.3. The serial.device in
 2.04/2.1 is faster and improved over the 1.3 serial.device. My serial
 port speed is set to 31,250. My terminal baud is set to 38,400. Serial
 port buffer is set to maximum(65K).I recommend using Baud Bandit v2.0
 Be sure RTS/CTS handshake is 'on' and SAVE your prefs to disk once you've
 set your serial port in Workbench and your baud rate and handshake in
 your terminal program. Actual performance will depend on what modem and
 software you are connecting TO and LINE QUALITY. This has worked the best
 for me. I hope it helps you increase your modem's cps rate as well as
 increase the number of connections you can make and give you more info
 on the connection you've just made.     Later......
