About:

3rd C-Net Pfile I've created. Simple ARexx adventure with multiple choices. Be
sure to make the right choice or death awaits you! 


Installation:

Pretty straight forward. After unarchiving the pfile, copy "ws2.rx" to
Pfiles: or Doors:. Adopt it as an ARexx file, and disable "more". That's it.
Not worthy of an AmigaGuide doc.
