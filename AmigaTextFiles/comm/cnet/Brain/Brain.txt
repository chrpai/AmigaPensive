    Introduction.
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 Brain is a thinking game for CNet, based on the Logo-clone Brain
 (written by André Wichmann). It's a conversion of the door BrainE!
 written by me (Morpheus) for Excelsior! BBS.

 The purpose of this game is to reproduce a randomly generated pattern
 by selecting the right squares in the right order.


    Installation.
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 The default-path where this door should be installed is pfiles:Brain/
 If you want to install it in a different directory then you will have
 to edit the Brain-script and change the 'path'-setting at the top of
 the script.

 Now go to the pfiles section on your BBS and add Brain as an ARexx door.
