 ECHOES.MED is a sound module made with MED 3.0- that was almost played 
 in public. Every year, my school holds a "battle of the bands". During
 my freshman year, some friends of mine were putting a band together,
 and agreed to let me play one of my computer songs (this one) as a part
 of their set- so when the day came to go on stage and perform, I
 strapped my Amiga onto my back and walked down to the field house, where
 the competition was being held. Unfortunately, one of my cable converters
 broke, and couldn't be replaced until the set was over.
