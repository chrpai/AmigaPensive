NAME
       Online

   SYNOPSIS
       Online

   FUNCTION
       Makes Zeus go online, to "wake up" the system and activate
       serial transfers.  Initialises online time to zero, sets
       logon time to the current time and tells the Line Watchers
       to go into online viewing mode.

       This is called immediately after a call has been answer,
       generally after MailerWait on an external/remote line.

   RESULT
