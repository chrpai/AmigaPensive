
Now you should think of a password to use for this BBS.  The password can
contain up to 16 characters (any ASCII-characters are allowed, not only
text).  Try to think of something that isn't too easy to guess, since you
probably don't want anyone else logging in using your account.  It is also
a good idea to change your password every now and then.  Don't forget to
write down your password somewhere.

Note: When you type in your password, you will not see the characters that
you actually typing, only '-' for each character.  This doesn't mean that
your password will consist of '-'-characters, only that you don't have to
worry about someone else looking at the screen while you are entering your
password.
