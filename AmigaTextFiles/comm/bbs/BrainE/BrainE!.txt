    Introduction.
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 BrainE! is a thinking game for Excelsior! BBS, based on the Logo-clone
 Brain (written by André Wichmann).

 The purpose of this game is to reproduce a randomly generated pattern
 by selecting the right squares in the right order.


    Installation.
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 The default-path where this door should be installed is Doors:BrainE!/
 If you want to install it in a different directory then you will have
 to edit the BrainE!-script and change the 'path'-setting at the top of
 the script.

 Now go to the doors area on your BBS and add BrainE! as an ARexx door.
