Registering through Gateway BBS gives you the following advantages:

* Access to many TransAmiga message and file areas on the BBS.
  You can download all the latest TransAmiga files, utilities,
  updates etc from local TransAmiga file areas and ones linked
  to the rest of the world, and read and write messages in 
  TRANSAMIGA, the official support conference, also linked to the 
  rest of the world.

* Access to TAGAMMA, where you can download the latest 'gamma'
  versions of TransAmiga made for registered users only - these 
  are usually released well before public release versions, and
  have many additional features and improvements.

* Direct support from the sysop on setting up and using the software

Upon receipt of your payment, I will create your personalised keyfile, and
send it to you by one of the methods listed below, usually within 24 hours.
