
    Welcome to The BABYLON PROJECT. This BBS System is one man's dream made
  manifest. It is the culmination (well,so far...) of 5 years of work that
  began as the result of frustration over the lack of adequate BBS systems
  for the Amiga computer. It has grown from a system on par with the likes
  of "Color 64" and "CNET 64/128" to a system that is fully capable of
  becoming a UseNet node, a FidoNet node, a REAL InterNet node, full handling
  of .QWK packet exchange, and currently I am working on implementation of
  direct ISDN line support. 
    The system is not without it's "growing pains". After all, this system
  represents a dream made manifest,trying to figure itself out. 
    The system is also not without a certain limited degree of intellegence.
  It has been deliberately created to constantly anticipate users needs.
    The BABYLON PROJECT represents our last,best hope for a viable,working
  BBS System well into the future. It's a port to call, a place to visit
  and meet new people. This is the culmination of the first of the BABYLON
  projects. The software is called ShadoeGate, the SysOp is Rick.
