This fabulous adventure was programmed in C. It includes over 250 items,
150 monsters and about 50 different spells and/or special effects. The
two current versions (v1.xx for Amigas and PC local use, and v2.xx for
PC BBS use) use a random dungeon design which is generated for each level
as the player enters it. As the levels get higher the treasures become
more plentiful and the monsters in turn become more powerful and harder
to defeat.
