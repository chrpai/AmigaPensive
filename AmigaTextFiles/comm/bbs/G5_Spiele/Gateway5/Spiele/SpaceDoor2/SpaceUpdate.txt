Changes for this version of SpaceDoor
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The most recent information is at the end of the file.

You should place this file online somewhere for players to read, since
much of the information is needed for them to play. Latest changes
are at the bottom of the file.

You should have the following files online for players to read, using
DF:
  SpacePlayer.txt        The player instructions
  SpaceUpdate.txt        This update file
  SpaceCargo.txt         Trading hints
  SD-Scores              Score file generated by SpaceScores
  SD-SecRpt              Initial sector report (generate this
                         short sector report with SpaceEd)
  
Creating a seperate SpaceDoor menu will make things a little
less cluttered. For your convenience, a DLG menu file is included,
you can copy it to your DLGCONFIG:MENU/ directory, and add a menu
entry to get to it somewhere on your BBS.

April 1991:
~~~~~~~~~~~
 - Fixed news files delete. In the previous version, players news files
   were not deleted, because of the space in the user's name. This
   now has quotes around it, and should work.

 - Changed the prices of all trade items, allowing much greater
   profits. With some careful trading, I racked up $10,000 in about
   40 turns. This should make the game more playable (and thus
   interesting).

 - Reduced the price of a fake ID from Pirates, and the price of
   clearing your name from Govt ships.

 - Changed the random function. I cannot get the timer function in
   Lattice to work (it gurus the machine every time if mungwall is
   running). So, instead of using timer to get the ticks to set the
   seed for the lrand48() function, I am using rand() for the seed.

 - Changed a bit of text (not much).


May 2, 1991:
~~~~~~~~~~~~
 - Changed the way a Discovery is moved, once a player discoveres it.
   Before, sectors would be read at random, until an empty one was
   found, and the Discovery would be placed there. Now, only up
   to 20 random sectors will be read, after which sectors will be
   read sequentially starting at 25 until an empty one is found.
   If the entire galaxy is scanned without finding an empty sector,
   the discovery is lost.
   
 - Now, with all sector and player read/writes, if the files are busy,
   SpaceDoor will wait 5 ticks and try again, until the file is free.
   This should make the game more secure on systems where multiple
   players could be playing, but results in an endless loop if the
   files arn't there!

 - Now, there is no longer any chance that an unknown item will
   completely destroy a ship, although there is still the (small)
   possibility of serious damage.


May 12, 1991:
~~~~~~~~~~~~~
You will have to re-generate your data files and start the game over
to use this version!

Delete Space:#?, then copy the entire contents of this archive to
Space:. Then use Space:SpaceEd to create the new data files.

 - Players can now buy turns at Government office, for $100 per
   turn, up to 99 turns.
   
 - Added a wanted list. This, available on Government Outposts,
   will provide a list of Wanted and Fugative players, along with
   their current location. This will help you if you are bounty
   hunting.
   
 - Changed the bounties. Now destroying a Wanted ship pays $2000,
   destroying a Fugative pays $5000. This will make bounty hunting
   a bit more profitable.
   
 - If you defeat another player's guard, it becomes yours, and you
   own the sector. This is an added incentive to fight guards rather
   than pay the toll.
   
 - You may now deploy guards in any sector except a Government one,
   or ones close to sector 1. This lets you own inhabited sectors.
   
 - The Police will now fire on any Wanted or Fugative ship trying
   to leave a sector. This makes trading in illegal items a little
   more dangerous. Note that you are probably safe until you sell
   the items.
   
 - Each time your discoveries reach 10, you get $500 times the
   number of battles you have won as a "battle bonus", but only if
   your status is currently Clean. You also still get a tech level
   increase, to a max of 10.
   
 - You cannot enter Government Offices or Ship Shops on Government
   Outposts unless your status is Clean.
   
 - You may use a Hyperwave Beacon as a Tracer. When you use a Hyperwave
   Beacon like this, you place it on a players ship. Then, at a later
   date, you may use another hyperwave beacon to get a list of all
   the ships that you have planted beacons on, and their current
   locations. Note that once you get this report, all the beacons
   you planted will be destroyed, so you can only get one report
   from each beacon that you plant. You can tell if there is
   a tracer on your ship, and who planted it, by viewing the
   info on your ship, from the info menu. You cannot trace system
   players.
   
   This means that there are now 3 things you can Use a hyperwave
   beacon for: A distress signal, plant as a tracer, or activate
   previously planted tracers. After selecting Use and your beacon,
   hit N until you get the option you want. You still need a beacon
   to Hail ships.
   
 - If your status is not Clean, when you hail a Pirate, he may ask
   you if you want to earn $20,000. If you answer yes, then you
   will have to take some classified documents to the Alien world
   that he specifies. Since this constitutes treason, it is not
   advisable to land on a Government Outpost until you make the
   delivery. If you are currently smuggling blueprints, this will
   show up in player info, on the info menu. If your status is Clean,
   the Pirate will not ask you if you want to do this. Note that you
   will need a translator to make the delivery. If you accept a
   delivery contract, and don't have a translator, then you are screwed
   because you won't be able to buy one.

 - If you land on an Alien planet where you are not delivering
   blueprints, then the WarLord of the planet will confiscate all of
   your cargo.

 - Removed the ANSI screen from SpaceEd. This was interesting the
   first time, but after that it became annoying.

 - Made a couple of other minor changes, but I can't remember what.
   You will have to play to see :)

Brian Fehdrau tells me that SpaceDoor will also work with Falcon. Here
is a quote from a message he sent me:
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Add something like this line to your config/menus.bbs file:

    MENU <whatever_menu>
         NORMAL S RAW - "spacedoor" "\"FULLNAME\""

That's about it.  If you're reading with an IBM character set, the characters
around the FULLNAME are the double-angle-bracket characters, something like
"<<FULLNAME>>".  They can be typed with alt-'(' and alt-')'.  It's important
that FULLNAME be in uppercase.

June 1991
~~~~~~~~~
Lots of changes this time. I hope these make the game a little more
interesting.

>>> Due to the lack of response I have gotten, this will probably be <<<
>>> the final version.                                               <<<

*** WARNING *** 

    I would like to stress again, that playing with the Sysop editor
    can severely mess up the data files if you edit sectors and players.
    I recommend that you ONLY change the system players, and only the
    items mentioned in SpaceSysop.txt.  You have been warned!

*** Once again, it is necessary to RE-CREATE THE GALAXY, since the old
*** data files are no longer compatible. The new editor is the same
*** except that it creates and uses the new files.

 - SpaceScores is no longer needed, SpaceDoor will generate and
   display the scores file when the user logs off. The player's
   current score is always available on the Info menu, along
   with the number of sectors owned. The score file is generated
   much more quickly than with SpaceScores, because the galaxy
   doesn't have to be scanned.

 - Fixed bug that would allow a player to take an object, then select
   Move, and enter the current sector number. The object would still
   be there, and he could take it again. This has been fixed. This
   also fixed a couple of other bugs.

 - Govt. ships will no longer be willing to clear your name if you are
   carrying classified blueprints to an alien world. This makes treason
   more dangerous.

 - Previously, if a player couldn't afford a toll, and fought and lost,
   then they were stuck in the sector with no cash, no way to leave,
   and no hope. Now wrecked ships are exempt from tolls (and the guards
   belonging to a wrecked ship won't collect tolls).

 - Battle bonus is now paid even if your status is not clean, but only
   if your ship is not wrecked.

 - Players with wrecked ships do not appear on the wanted list.

 - Now, when a player activates a tracer, it is not automatically
   removed. Instead, players may remove tracers at Govt. Offices
   in sectors 1 to 20. (for a price). Also fixed bug in printing
   traced players.

 - Added Computer option on main menu. Several options moved to this
   menu. The computer will find the closest sectors of a
   given type (costs 50 energy), or get info on any sector in
   the galaxy (cost $100 and 100 energy). The selections from
   the player info menu are now here too.

 - Now, when a player self destructs, he is deleted completely,
   and may start over right away. The entire galaxy must be scanned
   to remove any sectors the player owned, this takes time.

 - The random functions have been replaced, they are much better
   now. However, they still arn't perfect, but I am at a loss as
   to how to improve it. The new random function results in a small
   delay when landing.

 - To delete the players news file, DeleteFile() instead of c:delete
   is now used.

 - You no longer need 2 turns to land. I can't remember why I did this
   in the first place anyway. Now you can use your last turn to land.

 - There is now a 20 percent chance that a wandering black hole will
   move your ship to another sector when you are done. Before, this
   only happened if the sector was too full to hold more ships.
   There are no black holes in Govt. sectors, but if you get towed,
   you could run into one (see below).

 - Now, it costs $200 * tech level to park in a Govt. sector. This
   means that is will cost you to have the protection that a Govt.
   sector offers when you are logged off, and that more advanced players
   will have to pay more. If you don't pay, your ship is towed to
   another sector. If you run out of turns in a Govt. sector, you
   don't get a chance to pay, you just get towed.

 - Changed the way <CTRL-C> is handled. Previously, I declared my own
   CXBRK function, which is supposed to be a no-no. Now, I use the
   signal function to set the break trap. After one <CTRL>-C is
   received, the exit routine (which calculates scores, writes data
   files, cleans up, and exits) is called, and further <CTRL>-Cs are
   ignored. Previously, extra <CRTL>-Cs could cause problems with the
   data files (IE: data corruption) if they were received at the exact
   wrong time (I hope this was the problem, I checked EVERYTHING!).

 - You can no longer use a HyperJump unit if carrying illegal documents.
   You will have to deliver them the hard way! Also, the Alien Warlord
   will not fix your status any more, you will have to hail a Govt ship.

 - Changed the Find Owned function to display sector numbers as they
   are being scanned (for impatient users). This function also now
   stops at the end of the page and waits for the user to press a key
   before continuing.
