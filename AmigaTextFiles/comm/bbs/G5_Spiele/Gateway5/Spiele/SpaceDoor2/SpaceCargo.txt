  Following is a list of cargo, the planets on which they are
produced, and their base prices.

  When you buy cargo, you will usually get a better deal on
a planet that produces it. When you sell cargo, you will
usually get a better price for it on a planet that DOES
NOT produce it. So, for example, you would want to buy
Computers on an Industrial planet, and sell them
anywhere else. I say usually, because there is a random
factor built in.

  When you buy and sell cargo, the price is randomly
generated, based on the base price. When you trade
cargo on a planet that produces it, the actual price may
be either lower or higher than the base price. When you
trade cargo on a planet that does not produce it, the price
should almost always be higher than the base price.

  For cargo that is NOT produced on the planet on which you
trade it, the actual price may range from the base price up
to twice the base price.

  For cargo traded on planets which DO produce it, the price
may range from less than the base price up to twice the base
price.

  The quantity of a particular cargo available on a planet will
usually be much higher if that planet produces the cargo.

  The following table of what is produced where and base prices
should help you trade wisely. You may wish to buffer and
print it to refer to while playing.
