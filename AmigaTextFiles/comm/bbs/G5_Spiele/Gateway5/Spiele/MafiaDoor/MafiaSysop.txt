Ok, to set up the door:
 [1] Assign MAFIA: to wherever you want the game to be. This is best done
     in your startup-sequence, or in the script that starts your BBS.
 [2] Un-lharc or copy the files from the archive to MAFIA:
 [3] Copy Mafia.menu to your menus directory.
 [4] Edit your doors menu to include a key to go to the Mafia menu.
 [5] You may have to edit the Mafia menu key to return to the doors
     menu.

The game should now be ready to play, unless I left something out. You may
wish to archive the instructions and place them in your download, players
have little chance of survival if they don't read them.

To reset the game:
  DELETE MAFIA:#?.data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
There is nothing coded into the game to determine when someone wins, I
left this out to give sysops more control over when the game ends. Post
a bulletin or message defining your end of game rules. I suggest that the
first player to reach 5000 blocks wins. Then reset the game.

A small utility may (or may not) be available in the future to registered
sysops to change a few of the default values in the game. Such an editor
would not be able to edit players, but would allow you to change the
number of turns/day and what a player starts with.
