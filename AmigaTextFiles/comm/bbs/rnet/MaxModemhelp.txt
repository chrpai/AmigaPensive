Here's the settings you should have set up in the Modem Setup Optionarea of Maxs:MAX: 38400       MIN: 2400        FLAG  ON  Seven-wire Handshaking                                  FLAG  ON  Locked BPS Rate                                  FLAG OFF  Send String Twice                                  FLAG OFF  Slow Command String                                  FLAG  ON  Auto Connect StringOk, now for your modem's settings:AT &C1 &D2 &K3 \N3 W2The \N3 is very important it will force your modem to connect with aremote no matter what the remote (calling modem) modem's settings are.What it will do will try to connect at the best protocol possiblebetween the two modems.If \N is any value other than 3 it could potentially make your modemhang up on the calling modem depending on the calling modem's setting.&K3 puts your modem into the RTS/CTS flow control mode.W2 sends the *actual* connect rate between your modems to Maxs andnothing else.  This will prevent that garbage chars that your usersare seeing just prior to your modem hanging up on them.The best way to handle this is to boot up your Term prog ie JrCommor Terminus or whatever you're using...then when you get the OKmsg type in that command string above.AT&C1&D2&K3\N3W2 and hit returnyou should get another OK replynow type AT&W1 and hit return this will store the proper configurationto memory bank one of your modem.now whenever  you send an ATZ1 to your modem it will configurethe proper settings so by putting ATZ1 in the modem area initblock in Maxs your modem should be good to go.Lemme know if this works out for you <G>Egress-1
