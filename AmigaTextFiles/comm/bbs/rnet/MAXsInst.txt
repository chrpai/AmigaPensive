
Hi, ok here's an attempt at explaining how to set up the Fidonet
and Echo Areas/Distribution for MAXs.

Fidnonet Option Area

Rocket Net addresses will be 10:[AREACODE]/0-99  (0 = hub)

Place your Address in there...ie Mine is 10:806/0 
Kicker's will be 10:218/0
Enigma's will be 10:704/0

ORIGIN line...I just put my BBS name, number, and Location which
will be added to each post posted in the echo areas.

Turn on your ALLOW PICKUP and ALLOW SEND flags so that if any of
us choose to do an unscheduled poll we'll be able to.  You will
be required to poll a minimum of every other nite.

now, lets move on to the echo subs.  
Setup a Net Sysops sub this is a must
RSYSOPS 

the additional sub areas are optional and will be
a General Sub                RGENSUB
a Maxs Sysops Sub            RMAXSYS
a Debate Sub                 RGENDEB
a Amiga Sub                  RAMIGAS
a BBS software Sub           RBBSSFT

****See inclosed Echolist.txt file for all updated areas/echos****

You can name them anything you wish...the capitol letters represent
the AREATAG you'll use in the Distribution menu of MAXs

I highly recomend the Debate Sub and General Subs as a minimum but
what the hell why not run all of these to start?

You set these up just like your normal msg areas but make sure you
flag the echo on them and don't allow aliases to be used.
Take note of the section numbers these are...you'll need them for
the distribution area.

now on to the Distribution Setup.

In order here goes...

SECT:

Place the section number of the echo sub area here.
----------------------------------------------------

AREA:

This is tricky...do not use the Sub Name that you set up use the
AREATAG in the ECHOLIST I'm uploading that applies to the section
number for the sub.

For example, you create a section called Amiga as section # 20
the AREATAG will be RAMIGAS.
---------------------------------------------------------

DEST ADDRESS:

This will be MY address which is 10:100/1.0 what this will do will
have MAXs compile a pkt from this echo sub and address it to me so 
that when I call it'll transfer all posts to me and recieve all posts
from me of the same area and place them in your sub.  This also works
when/if you decide to call me with Fidonet and poll pkts.

If you plan on polling other BBS's or allowing other BBS's to poll 
you then you must make additional entries for the sub with their
net address as well.  For example since I'll be polling both 
Kicker's Hole and Enigma then I'll have to set up two entries for 
each echo area each with a different destination address.  We will
maintain a NODELIST (destination addresses) in the NODELIST file
I'm sending and maintaining. 
-------------------------------------------------------------------

PASSWORD:

use your password in this block. Password will be assigned upon
entry.
----------------------------------------------------------------

OPTADDRESS:

Disreguard this...put nothing here
----------------------------------------------------------------

OPT ORIGIN:

Disreguard this as well.......
---------------------------------------------------------------

FLAG BOTH LZH and OLD to on.

Save your configuration and we are/should be set to NET <G>

You must define the phone numbers in your fido autodialer to be
able to call out to another node.  Do this in your Distribution
area...use a psuedo section number for the section block, the 
psuedo section numbers are as follows, 101 for the first phone #
in your list, 102 for the second, 103 for the 3rd and so on.

Then leave the AREA blank but put the phones NET ADDRESS in the 
DEST: block...fill in the password to use and don't forget to flag
lzh and old.
