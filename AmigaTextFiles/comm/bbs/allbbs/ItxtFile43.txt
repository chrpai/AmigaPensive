       Name:  FreeForm BBS
Lead author:  Ken Schenke
Support BBS:  Fido 1:280/10 816-246-5253 (Currently Netmail only)

This new BBS software not only allows every config value imaginable to
be set/changed by the SysOp, but the entire design and flow-of-logic
that operates the bbs can also be altered.

It will eventually be able to emulate menus/msg-bases/file-bases/etc
of all known (42) Amiga BBS software packages (as well as those of
your own design).

SWHQ is currently beta-testing this software.
