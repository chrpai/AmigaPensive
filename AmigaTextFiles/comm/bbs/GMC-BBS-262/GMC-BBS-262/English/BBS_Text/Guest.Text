Welcome to my mailbox. In the following you find some essential infomation for
the handling of the box.

The basic idea is to give my users the chance to have always the latest
software in Public Domain or Shareware. Moreover there is the possibility
to exchange ideas about programs or something like this. Ideas to create
new message boards will certainly be accepted and executed.

To get the benefit of loading programs or reading messages and so on you need
to join as a member in this box. There ist no fee, you only need to fill in a
form with your personal data and then you can start. (Main Menu ===> JM = Join
as a Member). Any kind of contribution will certainly be accepted.

When you fill in the form completely your status changes from Guest to New User
so that you can download some files from the file system, read and write
messages, send and receive private mails. It will take me 1-2 days to check
your application and if it was filled in correctly your status changes to
Valid User. From now on you have access to all public areas.

It is possible to abort long listings or text files with `Esc' or `^C'. or to
stop them with `Space' or `P'. Exeption: The Help Text can not be aborted or
stopped.

I hope you join as a member in my box and pass on this number to other amiga
users. If you have some questions concerning the program you can call me with
CS = Chat SysOp or leave me a private mail.
Have a nice day and a clear telephone line.
