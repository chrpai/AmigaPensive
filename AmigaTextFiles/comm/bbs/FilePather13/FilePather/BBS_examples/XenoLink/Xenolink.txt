
 How to use FilePather with Xenolink:
 ====================================

 If you want FilePather to process files with other suffixes than '.LHA'
 then just add the suffix you want (.LZX .LZH .TXT) etc. The text inside
 should be equal to the .LHA script (except the "test archive" bit), just
 make sure that there exists a correct extract, update environment table
 for that specific archiver (Env:FilePather/.xxx_(update|extract) and
 everything should be fine!
