
 How to use FilePather with TEMPEST BBS:
 =======================================

 Simply change your add-scripts (add_lha; add_zip; add_(txt|nfo|...)
 to the following (as you won't need any more bbs-adds):

  Filepather FILE %s

 Thats all! Output will be redirected to the Tempest BBS screen
 automatically.
