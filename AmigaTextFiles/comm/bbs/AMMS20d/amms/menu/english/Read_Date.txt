Format of date :

 x = Add x days to actuell day
     (Examples : 0=today, -1=yesterday, 1=tomorrow)

 D.M = Day D and month M of thies year (also D-M)
       (Examples in the year 1993 : 1.1=1.1.1993, 20-3=20.3.1993)

 D.M.Y = Day D in month M of the year Y (also D-M-Y)
	 (Examples : 1.1.93, 31-12-93)

Type empty date to abort.
