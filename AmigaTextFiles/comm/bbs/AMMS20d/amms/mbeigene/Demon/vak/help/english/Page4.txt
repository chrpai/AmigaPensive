                             Function 1 : Edit

With this Option you can edit your VK or you can make a new one.
If you have a VK, it will be shown and you may ask if you really
want to change your VK.

Remember: You have only 6 lines for your VK.

When you make a new VK, the Editor ask two times for something, that is
not important for you.:-) Press RETURN twice.

The Editor is the normal AMMS-Full-Screen-Editor.
Press CTRL+H for Help. For end writing use the keys CTRL+Z.

Call:
Menu : with "E" or with the Cusorkeys.
CLI  : with VAK/Edit.
