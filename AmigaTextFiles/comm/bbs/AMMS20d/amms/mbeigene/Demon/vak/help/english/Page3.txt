                                 General :

In the first two lines you can see some informations about
you and the VAK. In the first line your username, the time you have left
and your level. In the second line the "Mode". This means the function
you are using or informations about Usershow and so on. At the VKs the
number after the ":" shows you how many VKs are ready to read, VK shows
you if you have a VK or not.

You can use the VAK in three ways.
1. Menu : You type the letter for the function to reach it.
2. Cusorkeys : With the Cusorkeys you go to a function (the selected is
               shown invers) and press Return.
3. CLI-Options : (only Avaible from the commandline-mode of the BBS)
                 You call the vak like "VAK/Show" to reach functions.
