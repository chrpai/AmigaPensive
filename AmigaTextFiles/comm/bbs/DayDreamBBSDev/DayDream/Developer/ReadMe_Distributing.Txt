
          Some guidelines for distributing DayDream doors/utils.

NOTE:   Theese  rules are not necessarily obeyed, but they give the users a
        small feeling of regularity.  :-)


· Always create a DayDream/ directory inside the archives with necessary
  subdirectories.   ie  if  you  are  distributing  a  door you would need the
  DayDream/Doors/  DayDream/Docs/  and  possibly DayDream:Configs/ directories
  inside your archive.  (This avoids messy archives and stripped files)

· Always try to include the following topics in your documentation:
  · What kind of door/util is this ? What good does it make ?
  · How to install and use this util/door. As extensive as possible.
  · Contact possibilites. (BBS number, email or snail-mail address)
  · What happens to my system if I use this utility.  (Does it alter my
    userbase, up/downloadlog etc.)

· Required configuration.  OS version, processor, DayDream & dreamdoor.library
  version, libraries needed etc.

· Mention  any  kinds  of  problems  there  may be when using/installing this
  util/door and how to correct or workaround them.


                                  Thank you!
