
The biggest changes from Blackmar's Dungeon and Dungeon 2.0 are as follows:

The bank now compounds interest by 10 percent for every day since the last
time the caller logged in. (i.e. if it has been 3 days since the last time
you've called, it will compound your account by 30%) It would have been more
realistic to have it compound daily, but we figured we needn't get that
realistic. Thus it is no longer necessary to call every day to get your account
compounded by 10%. (A strategy that several users on my BBS were using.)

Many users on my system complained that it was too easy to get killed after you
had logged off. This frustraited many players to the point of no longer playing
the game. Many clamored for SOME way of protecting themselves between calls.
Well, now you can. Players can now cast a Sphere of Invulnerability on
themselves before you quit the game that will protect you from attackers while
you are gone. When englobed, your character can not be attacked by another
player nor can he have his gold stolen. This protection does not come without a
price, however, and the price is a large one. It costs 10 Gems to cast a
sphere, but it will last until the next time you log in. The Sphere has no
other effect on the game. It will NOT protect you from any fights you happen to
start while playing the game.

The ability to drink more than 5 potions has been added. Keeping with the
tradition of giving a few extra points for drinking more than 1 potion at a
time, you can now drink 10 potions for 120 hit points, and 20 potions for 260
hit points. Of course, you must HAVE 10 or 20 potions and you must be able to
gain at least 110 or 240 hit points to be able to drink them. This is very
handy for high level characters who have dropped low on hits.

You can now teleport straight to the Store for double the cost of teleporting
to the north dungeon. 6 Gems.

The game is now date independant. Meaning the game now automatically
resets at midnight without the need to copy a file called DungeonTurn.File to
your Dungeon directory. This feature was put in by Chris Timmerberg. We've
expanded on this idea in the following way:

If you cast Levitate and then play for 50 moves and then quit the game to play
the other 100 (assuming the sysop has it set up for 150 moves) later in the
day, when you log back in you will still be levitating as long as the date
hasn't changed. In other words, levitation, once cast, lasts all day. You can
quit and re-enter the door as much as you want throughout the day and you will
still be levitating.

Sphere of Invulnerability works the same way.

Dead players are now listed by name with the word (dead) appearing next to the
name. I've always wanted to know who it was that was laying there on the
ground.

There are now 3 (Count 'em!) ways to move your character in Castle Dungeon. The
traditional , . m and / keys still work the same and now include the numeric
keypad keys 4 5 6 and 8 as well as the keyboard keys T F G and V. The display
has been updated to reflect such.

ANSI support for keeping the pictures on the screen during fights has been
added. For some reason, Dungeon 2.00 did not have this ability.

What The Future Holds
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There are a couple features from Blackmar's Dungeon that we haven't implemented
in Castle Dungeon yet, but plan on adding. Look for the next version to
contain:

A Newsfile -- Will keep a running track of who's killed who, who's ahead in the
dungeon, who's killed by what monster, etc.

More Spells -- You can never have enough of these. Send your suggestions
via USENET EMail (les@lppl.mi.org) or by calling the number posted eleswhere
in the doc files.

More Monsters -- See above. ;-)

More Magic Items -- Plans include adding Boots of Stealth which would allow you
to sneak past monsters and hide from people with a wisdom greater than 4. This
is only one of a few magic items under consideration.

A Goal -- Currently this game doesn't really have a goal. Suggestions for one
are being taken. Right now the best we've come up with is a Space Empireish
"First To Reach Level 100" type goal. 

An Expanded Dungeon -- We do plan to make it larger.

Greater ANSI Support -- Eventually, we may try to design a screen like what
Chris had planned for Dungeon 3.00.

Thats it for now. Any and all suggestions/comments are welcome. There may be a
couple features I neglected to mention as we added quite a few and smashed a
lot of bugs. (and corrected a lot of spelling errors) New releases will be put
out through ADS and my BBS system.
