|                  Help file on playing Castle Dungeon                    | 
|_________________________________________________________________________|

Castle Dungeon is a interactive game where you can walk through
a dungeon and fight other users and monsters.  There are treasures
in the dungeon such as better swords, shields and hit point potions.

Map:  A map is provided showing the dungeon. It shows the store and
the starting point.  

Hit Points:  This is a number representing your characters health.
When you get to 0 you are dead and are kicked out of the dungeon.
You may simply re-enter the dungeon and you will be reincarnated to
the starting values.  The store will not sell you a hit spells if
your health is above your max.  Your max is determined by how good
your weapons are.  You can carry hit potions and use them as needed,
40 is the maximum that the store will sell you.  
   
Monsters:  Some people feel is best to run from the tougher monsters
until you have at least a sword and shield of 2 or 3.  The tuffer the
monster, the better chance of getting more gold from them when you kill
them.  Monsters and treasures are randomly place every time you enter 
the dungeon.  The uncharted dungeon has some different monsters that
are even tougher.  All monsters get tougher as you get tougher.

Other Players:  You can fight other players.  If you kill them, you get
their gold, and if they have a better shield or sword you get those also.
You can not fight other users if they are very much weaker than you.
The defender can drink hit potions and eat magic gems during a battle
to increase his hit points. 

Running:  You have a 50/50 chance per attempt to run from monsters or
other players.  These odds can be modified by finding agility potions.
If you are unsuccessful in the attempt the monster will take a swing at you.

Uncharted Dungeon:  In this part of the dungeon, rooms are not marked.
This is a good place to hide from other users.

Deep Dungeon:  This part of the dungeon has the best treasures, and is
the most dangerous.  It takes a Gold Key to enter the Deep Dungeon.

Magic Gems:  You can do various magic with magic gems.  They can only be
found in the uncharted.  As your Wisdom gets higher you have a better
chance of being sucessful with gem magic. Magic gems can also be purchased at
the store.

Armor:  Better armor can be found in the uncharted.  There are 4 types of
armor, chain mail, leather armor, plate mail and royal plate mail.

Compass:  You have a compass showing you your direction, except when deep
in the Deep Dungeon where magic causes your compass to spin.

Bank:  A bank is provided at the store.  You money is safe there even if
you die.  Also money in the bank can not be stolen. Interest for the bank is
compounded daily.

Sphere Of Invulnerability:  With 10 Gems, you can cast a Sphere of
Invulnerability that will protect you from other players inbetween game
sessions. It only protects you against attacks from other players and does not
affect fights you get into while playing. It will last until the next time you
play the game. If you quit the game and then play again later the same day,
the sphere will still be active. The same is true of levitation.

Stealing Money:  A Magic Sphere can be found in the Deep Dungeon.  This
magical item is used to steal money from other users.

Stats:  Strength adds to your chances of hitting and also affects how much
damage you do.  Wisdom helps in doing gem magic and with a Wisdom greater than
4 you can hear people in adjacent rooms.  Agility increases you chances of
running from battles.

Sickness:  If your character becomes ill, you can return to the store for
a cure.

Movement: There are three forms of movement in the dungeon. M = Move Forward
, = Rotate Left  . = Rotate Right and / = Turn around 180. If you prefer you
can use the keypad keys 8 4 6 and 5 to match the functions above,
respectively. In addition, you can use the T F G and V keys to move in a
compass direction. T = North  F = West  G = East  and V = South. This is for
folks who prefer that form of movement.

Good luck
