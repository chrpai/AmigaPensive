DLG HyperTyper v2.20 - Action doorgame for use with DLG Pro v1.0

DLG HyperTyper v2.20 is a doorgame in which the user must type appearing
words as fast as he/she can. Fast typers get more bonus than slower typists
and playing the game often will surely improve your typing skills! When
playing, the difficulty level will increase every five words, so gameplay
is quite fast! 

There is also a battlemode available, in which 2 nodes on your BBS can play
against each other! This and all the extra features included will keep your
users happy for a long time!

Shareware fee $10, which will remove the BBS Advertisement screens and
will fully enable the statistics view option.
