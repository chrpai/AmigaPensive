training in martial arts.  It is advisable to pick a city far away from any
other, but not so far that it takes a TON of movement points to get where
you are going.  Corporation/company cities also have level advancements,
weapon & armor shops, and the International Training Centers.

Once you chose a main base, it cannot be changed until the entire game
is finished!
