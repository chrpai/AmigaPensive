Companies

Companies in the world of Assassin rule the world - they are constantly
looking for new assassins to 'knock off' other companies leaders, or 
assassins.   They pay highly for accomplished missions, however, you will
be given tough assignments!  

    o Each company starts with a certain amount of money which is used
      as payment for accomplished missions.
 
    o Each company has a certain number of executives, which are trying to
      be killed by other companies' assassins.

If all the executives in a company are killed, the player's company will
recieve all 90% of the leftover assetts, with the assassin recieving 10%.

If a company runs out of cash, and executives are left, it will declare 
bankruptsy and cease to exist!

Once enrolled in a company the player signs a waiver to take out 50% of his
earned funds (bank money and pocket money) if he wishes to quit or join 
another company.  Make sure your first choice is your best choice! You need
more than $2000 in combined bank and personal savings to change your 
company.

The city which the company is in will have training centers and 
weapon/armor shops for the employee to use.
