Appendix A

Software Support


Technical support and help for your Zeus Bulletin Board System can be obtained in a variety of different ways. Support is available from your local distribution site and from the authors direct. A network feed for ZeusNet can be obtained from your local distribution site. If this proves to be too costly, or inconvenient, feeds can supplied via FidoNet to Internet email tunnelling, or via Internet email mailing lists.

The public ZeusNet echoes are currently: 

 TagName     | Description
 ------------+----------------------------------------------
 ZEUS_BETA   | Bug reports relating to Zeus
 ZEUS_SYSOP  | General Zeus-related chat
 ZEUS_UTIL   | Door/Utility programming for Zeus
 ZEUS_SCRIPT | ARexx script help and discussion
 ZEUS_USER   | Open for users of Zeus BBSs to chat


To subscribe to the Internet mailing lists, please visit the Zeus Developments website. In the Support section you will find details for subscribing.

When contacting us, please provide your Zeus serial number as obtained from the "About" option on the main Zeus control screen.
