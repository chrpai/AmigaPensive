Uploads to this BBS are accomplished using a standard file transfer protocol, 
which ever one you have selected in your terminal parameters.  Most files
should be COMPACTED and bundled together by a public domain archival utility
such as ARC or ZOO.  Once you have uploaded your file, you will be credited by
a multiple of the size of the file.  These upload credits can be used toward
downloads from the BBS.

Please do not upload pirated software.  Only freely-distributable software is
permitted on this BBS.  Also, any files in the downloader are, to the best of
my knowledge, either public domain or shareware.
