
  ToyJoin v1.0 by Joo/Toytools                                      Menu Usage

  o Use cursor keys or numbers to move in the menu.
  o Pressing [enter] joins the conference under the selector. (if access)
  o Press [esc] (twice) or [q] to quit the menu.
  o Pressing [i] displays some info about the conferences.
  o Pressing [help] or [h] displays this help page.

  Press any key to return to the menu!
